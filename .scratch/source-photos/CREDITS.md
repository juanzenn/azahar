# Source photography — provenance and licence

The full-resolution originals behind `public/images/`. They live here rather than in `public/`
because `output: "export"` copies all of `public/` into the deployed build, and an unreferenced
original is weight every customer would download.

Every photograph is from **Unsplash**, under the [Unsplash
License](https://unsplash.com/license): free for commercial use, no permission or attribution
required. Attribution is recorded anyway — it costs nothing and it is what makes a licence claim
checkable later.

The **fills** column is the map: which original became which slot. Each was cropped to its slot with
a cover fit, gravity on the most salient region, and written as mozjpeg at quality 82 — products at
**900×1200**, category heroes at **1200×900**, the dimensions ticket 03's placeholders established
and every layout downstream was designed against. Matching them exactly is what makes replacing a
photograph a file swap with no layout shift and no code change.

Two slots needed framing by hand, because the automatic gravity preferred a prettier region over the
one holding the thing being sold:

- `products/rosa-preservada.jpg` — cropped to the source's lower middle (left 10%, top 25%, width
  79%, height 73%) before the fit, or the gravity frames the bouquet standing behind the dome.
- `products/planta-verde.jpg` — gravity forced to the bottom edge, or the pot is cropped away.

Eight photographs below are unused. They came with the ticket-03 pool and lost their slot to a closer
match — mostly orchids, of which the pool held five against the catalog's two orchid slots. They are
kept because a taste call is worth being able to revisit.
